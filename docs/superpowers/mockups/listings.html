About this home

Rare opportunity to secure a premium 100 ft frontage lot in one of Mississauga's most coveted lakefront communities. This expansive ~100 × 170 ft property offers a unique setting, with approximately half of the rear lot backing onto the lush green space of Meadow Wood Park and the other half onto a neighbouring extended lot — a rare blend of privacy and established surroundings.

Ideally located just steps from Lake Ontario, the scenic trails of Rattray Marsh Conservation Area, Meadow Wood Tennis Club, and Meadow Wood Lakefront Park, this location offers an exceptional lifestyle surrounded by nature, recreation, and prestigious homes — all within a top-tier school catchment.
